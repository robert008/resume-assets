Face Recognition SDK

Register + Recognize in ~500ms

Complete face recognition solution running entirely on-device. Migrated from server-grade AI models to mobile with no accuracy loss. Supports both iOS and Android through Flutter FFI.

  • Face registration and recognition workflow
  • ~500ms processing time per face
  • Works completely offline
  • C++ core with Flutter plugin wrapper
